Pasadena’s taco spot

Taqueria Cerro de la Silla is a food truck at 2783 Red Bluff Rd in Pasadena, TX. Reviewers describe a sweet lady running the truck solo, fast service, fair prices and portions big enough to share. Regulars mention coming back weekly for the fajita tacos, pupusas and chicken tortas, and ordering the charro beans every time.
