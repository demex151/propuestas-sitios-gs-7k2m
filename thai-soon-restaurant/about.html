A family restaurant

Guests say Thai Soon is the oldest Thai restaurant in Dallas. The owner’s mother started it decades ago, and today she carries it on. It began as a completely vegetarian restaurant and still serves chicken as its only meat, with huge portions that regulars have driven for over 15 years to get.
