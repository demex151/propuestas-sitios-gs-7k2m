E&A Tires

E&A Tires is a tire and service shop on Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Highway in Mexia, Texas. Steven and his crew handle tires, alignments, front-end work and oil changes, and customers mention the kindness and honesty — even staying past closing to fix a flat.
