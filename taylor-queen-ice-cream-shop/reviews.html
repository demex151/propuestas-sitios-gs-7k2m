Taylor Queen is a staple of Taylor, MI. This ice cream shop has delicious sundae’s, variety of flavored soft serve and hard scoop, blizzards, slushes and even some unique popsicles that are normally from an ice cream truck. Very accommodating with special requests, add ons, take offs, you dream it they make it. Taylor Queen stays open longer in the season than other ice cream shops and later at night. The line moves quickly despite how it looks. The strawberry shortcake Sunday is the best!
