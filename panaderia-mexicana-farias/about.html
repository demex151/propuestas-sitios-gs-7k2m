Panadería Mexicana Farias

Panadería Mexicana Farias is a small pick-up bakery in Cottonwood, Arizona. Visitors stopping before a hike and locals alike praise the homemade pan dulce, tamales and breakfast burritos, friendly service and very reasonable prices.
