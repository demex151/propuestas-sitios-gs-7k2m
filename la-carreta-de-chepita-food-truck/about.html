About

La Carreta de Chepita

La Carreta de Chepita is a Honduran food truck on West 42nd Street in Odessa, Texas. Guests — even visitors from Denver — rave about the pollo con tajadas, pastelitos de carne molida, soups and the warm, welcoming service.
