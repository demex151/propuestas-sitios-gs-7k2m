★★★★★
I visited Krust Bakery Cafe with friends from my Meetup group after an earthing walk at Redondo Beach. The cafe is cozy and cute, perfect for relaxing after a walk by the ocean. We ordered a variety of sandwiches, including their signature Avocado Toast, the Galbi Sandwich, and a few other kinds. Everything was so delicious and full of flavor! The latte was also fresh and satisfying. We enjoyed it so much that we decided to make earthing a monthly activity and chose Crust Cafe as our go-to spot. Highly recommend!

★★★★★
I stopped by the bakery cafe on a Sunday. The store is neat and service was very friendly. There was a decent selection of baked goods, and a sandwich menu. There are seatings both inside and a a couple of tables outside in the shade. I enjoyed the breakfast sandwich was nicely warmed up with melt cheese and soft egg. The croissant, however, was disappointing. It has an unconventional curvy shape and lacks layers and flakes.
★★★★★
As a huge pastry lover, I’m always searching for a high quality bakery! I’m happy to say that Krust exceeded my expectations. They have an amazing selection of pastries (especially first thing in the morning) so it’s very challenging to make a decision 😄 Luckily, you can’t go wrong!
