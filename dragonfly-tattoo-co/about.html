Dragonfly Tattoo Co.

Dragonfly Tattoo Co. is artist Kari’s tattoo studio on West Maumee Street in downtown Adrian, Michigan. Clients describe thorough consultations, excellent linework, walk-in specials and a clean, cute, “groovy” shop where everyone feels at home.
