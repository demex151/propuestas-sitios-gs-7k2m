Backwoods Tattoo

Backwoods Tattoo Studio is a custom tattoo shop on South Lincoln Avenue in Lakeview, Michigan. Artists Trenton and Matt work in realism, traditional, ornamental, script, geometric and fine line, and clients praise the professionalism, cleanliness and how comfortable they feel in the chair.
