Matt & Russell

The owners are the barbers.

Brazos Barbershop is new ownership in a downtown Austin location that has been cutting hair for decades. Matt and Russell own the shop and cut every head themselves, and customers describe it as clean, relaxed and fairly priced for the Austin market.

Customers say the shop is clean and relaxed, with mirrors all over, and that the price is fair for downtown Austin.
