El Chef Latino

El Chef Latino is a family food truck at a food truck park on West Irlo Bronson Memorial Highway in Kissimmee, Florida. Regulars come back several times a week for yaroa con chicharrón, fried fish dinners, empanadas and fresh drinks served by owners who make everyone feel welcome.
