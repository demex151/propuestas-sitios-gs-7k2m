Mean Ink Tattoo

Mean Ink Tattoo is a tattoo and piercing shop on South Wayne Road in Wayne, Michigan. Artists like Brandon, Irish and Sarah do custom pieces, color, black-and-grey, cover-ups, lettering and piercings, and clients describe a laid-back crew with great prices.
