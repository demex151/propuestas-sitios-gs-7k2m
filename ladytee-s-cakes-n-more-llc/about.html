LadyTee’s Cakes N’ More

LadyTee’s Cakes N’ More is a pink-walled dessert shop on East Church Street in Sandersville, Georgia. Regulars say Lady Tee greets everyone with a “welcome in,” even from the back, and that the cakes, dipped strawberries and lunch counter keep them coming back.
