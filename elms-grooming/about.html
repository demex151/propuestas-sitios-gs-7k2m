ELM’s Grooming

ELM’s Grooming is a dog grooming shop on West Main Street in Royse City, Texas. Elicia and the team take time with every dog — sensitive skin, big breeds and first-timers — and owners from Royse City and Fate call it a quality-over-quantity hidden gem.
