The Scoop

The Scoop Ice Cream Shoppe is a welcoming ice cream parlor on South Main Street in Charleston, Missouri. Guests love tasting their way through the original flavors, the ice cream nachos and the homemade waffle chips — a favorite stop for locals and travelers alike.
